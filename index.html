Do any of these sound like you?

EnergyI'm always tired.

Even after a full night's sleep. Even when you sit down to rest, you feel guilty for it.

ClarityI'm overwhelmed.

Busy from morning to night, and still feeling you did nothing that mattered.

ExecutionI procrastinate.

You know what to do. You delay, you start over every Monday, and you call it a discipline problem.

These feel like three problems. As an engineer, I see them as symptoms. The bug is underneath, and more hours won't fix it.

Mum to mum

Children don't follow a schedule.

They fall asleep late, wake up early, need you the moment you open your laptop, and play happily alone the moment you close it. And somehow they always want your computer, your notebook and your pen. As a mum, I understand completely. No plan survives that. What matters is keeping your cool through all of it, without letting time overwhelm you. That's what Time Architecture is for.

Why it's not a time problem

Results = Time × Energy × Clarity × Execution

Most mums building a business get a working window of maybe 3 hours a day, often late at night. Two mums with the same 3 hours can get completely different results. When energy, clarity or execution is close to zero, even a full working window gives you almost nothing, and stealing more hours from sleep only makes it worse.

And consistency isn't something you're missing. It's what shows up when energy, clarity and execution work together.

What we'll do together

Why two mums with the same working window get completely different results. Then a 2-minute breath together, so you feel the difference right away.

Your life's plate

50–80 min

Fix the leak

Simple tools for each layer.

Energy: daylight, sleep, breath, movementClarity: one main task, no switchingExecution: focused blocks, batching, check-ins
80–105 min

Design your week

Put your hardest work in your peak window, batch the rest, and learn how to redesign it when the kids change everything.

105–110 min

Find the wake-up window that works for your body. You get a page to track it.

110–120 min
